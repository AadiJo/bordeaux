The geometry tool now searches for faster Bezier timing without leaving the authored corridor or crossing modeled field and strategy obstacles.

Context

The user reported that the original refinement looked unnatural and cut through areas they intended to avoid. The implementation therefore prioritized route intent and footprint safety over unconstrained geometric movement, while retaining the corrected fixed-path time optimizer as the timing authority.

Implemented result

  • Path-local, validated rectangular keep-outs that participate in shared analysis and persist with the path.
  • Dense worker-side robot-footprint checks for field bounds, HUB solids, barriers, portals, authored keep-outs, and TRENCH height.
  • An exact user-entered clearance requirement; unsatisfied searches return the authored path unchanged.
  • Five deterministic handle-length starts plus local refinement inside the authored per-segment corridor.
  • Explicit Draw, Remove, Save, Search, Dismiss, and Apply states; saving a safety region does not depend on finding a faster curve.

Observed behavior

With collision avoidance set to 0 m, the current LeftAuto2 fixture found a bounded refinement from 12.121 s to 12.000 s with a maximum centerline shift of 0.045 m during a read-only benchmark. Its existing 0.032 m obstacle clearance does not satisfy the safer 0.05 m default, so that setting intentionally returns unchanged rather than silently accepting less clearance.

Claim boundary: the result is the fastest valid candidate found within 72 deterministic evaluations of fixed-direction Bezier handles. It is not a global-optimality certificate.

Next phase
